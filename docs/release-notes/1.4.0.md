## What's new in 1.4.0

- **RiseUp servers.** The VPN search dialog now has two tabs, VPN Gate and RiseUp. The RiseUp tab lists the RiseupVPN gateways (Paris, Amsterdam, Montreal, Miami) and adds the one you pick as an OpenVPN connection. Available on Windows, Linux and Android.
- **Find the best server.** Each tab has its own checkbox. When it is on, the app measures the latency of the nearest servers, shows it in milliseconds, puts the fastest first and offers an "Add best server" button. It never adds or connects by itself.
- The RiseUp client certificate is short lived. When it expires, add the VPN again from the RiseUp tab.
- Translated into the 9 other languages.
